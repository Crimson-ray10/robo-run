How to Play

  • Build Program: Drag blocks into the workspace to sequence moves, turns, and loops.
  • Timer Countdown: Construct and execute your winning algorithm before time runs out.
  • Collision Recovery: If the robot hits a wall, you get a 1-minute recovery window to debug your code.
  • Reach the Flag: Reach 🏁 to unlock the next mission and earn up to 3 stars!
⌨️

Desktop Shortcuts

  • 1 2 4 Animation Speed (1x, 2x, 4x)
